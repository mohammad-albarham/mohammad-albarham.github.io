Asyad Global Logistics Challenge - Kaggle Competition

Nov 2021 Dubai, UAE

Asyad Group (Kaggle Days Meetup Dubai)

Placed 2nd in a live Kaggle Days competition estimating future ship prices for Asyad Group.

  • Python
  • AI
  • Kaggle-Competitions
  • Data Science
6 highlights
  • Finished 2nd on the final leaderboard as team "ninja" with a score of 0.06930, between the winning team at 0.06221 and third place at 0.07393.
  • Awarded a USD $1,500 gift certificate at the prize ceremony on 3 November 2021.
  • Built a regression model estimating future ship prices from historical maritime and shipping data, the challenge set by Asyad Group.
  • Competed live and on-site over the two-day event rather than as a remote, long-running Kaggle competition.
  • Held at the Dubai Exhibition Centre, Expo 2020 Dubai, on 2–3 November 2021.
  • Part of Kaggle Days Meetup Dubai, powered by Asyad Group together with Kaggle, LogicAI, and Hypermotion Dubai.

ICPC — JU Competitive Programming Contest (JUCPC)

Jun 2019 Amman, Jordan

ICPC — International Competitive Programming Contest & the University of Jordan

Competed in the 2018 and 2019 editions of the JU Competitive Programming Contest, earning the Extreme Programmers award and a First to Solve Problem A award in 2019.

  • C++
  • Algorithms
  • Data Structures
  • Problem Solving
6 highlights
  • Awarded the Extreme Programmers Certificate of Achievement at the 2019 JU Competitive Programming Contest on 22 June 2019.
  • Awarded a First to Solve Problem A Certificate of Achievement at the same contest, for being first in the field to get problem A accepted.
  • Both certificates were signed by William B. Poucher, ICPC Executive Director, and Ossama M. Ismail, ACPC Championship Director.
  • Also competed in JUCPC 2018 on 6 April 2018, receiving a Certificate of Participation signed by the Dean of the King Abdullah II School for Information Technology and the President of the Jordan Programmers Association.
  • Solved algorithmic problems in C++ under ICPC rules: three contestants to a team, one shared computer, five hours, penalty time for wrong submissions.
  • Organised by the University of Jordan with the Jordanian Programmers Association, the Students' Union, the Innovation and Entrepreneurship Center, and the ICPC Foundation.

IEEE RAS Portal: FireFighting I

Jun 2019 Amman, Jordan

IEEE RAS - University of Jordan Student Branch

Placed 4th and took the Best Design and Clean Sheet awards with a self-built firefighting robot, as team Electrone X.

  • Robotics
  • Embedded Systems
  • Mechanical Design
  • 3D Printing
6 highlights
  • Finished 4th overall in the IEEE RAS Portal: FireFighting competition, held on 29 June 2019 at the University of Jordan.
  • Won the Best Design Award for the mechanical design of the robot, and the Clean Sheet Award for completing the runs without faults.
  • Competed as team Electrone X with my colleague Abdullah Abu Dabaseh, each of us building and running a robot of our own design.
  • Built the robots from scratch on a custom four-wheel chassis, with microcontroller-driven motors and a servo, a motor-driven fan to put out the flame, and laser-cut acrylic and 3D-printed parts.
  • Certificates signed by Prof. Lutfi Al-Sharif, IEEE RAS Jordan Chapter Chairman, and Omar Turkmani, IEEE JU RAS Chairman.
  • Placed 3rd with the same colleague in the previous Portal: Firefighting competition, held in October 2018.
